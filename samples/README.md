# Theme samples

Five themes taken from real talks by Ivan Lutsenko. They are reading material for a new deck theme, not presets. Rules for a new theme are in [deck theme](../references/slides/theme.md).

Each folder holds:

- `README.md`: the occasion, the idea, the signature and the lessons from the original;
- `theme.css`: the theme rewritten as a Craft `deck-theme.css`, with the font files left out;
- `original-cover.png`: the cover of the original deck;
- `harold-prompt.txt`: the brief for the Harold picture;
- `harold-original.jpg`: the Harold from the original deck, where there was one. Older pictures have the meme lines baked in; a new picture leaves them out.

Samples:

- [newyear-cyberpunk](newyear-cyberpunk/README.md): New Year as Cyberpunk 2077. Neon on night navy, a garland, snow.
- [tsagan-sar](tsagan-sar/README.md): Tsagan Sar, the Mongolian lunar New Year. Cream paper, burgundy and gold ornament.
- [space](space/README.md): a launch or Cosmonautics Day. Deep navy, star field, sand headings.
- [blueprint](blueprint/README.md): no holiday. An engineering drawing for a technical deep dive.
- [ember](ember/README.md): no holiday. A calm dark pitch with one warm gradient.

The originals are at <https://ivan.nullptr.party>. Fonts in the samples come from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, except the Cyberpunk and OCR-A faces of the New Year sample, which the author supplies.
