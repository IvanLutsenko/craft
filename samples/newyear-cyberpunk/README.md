# New Year x Cyberpunk 2077

Talk: "CI/CD: a love story", December. The holiday met the game the audience knew, and the deck looked like a neon city on New Year's Eve.

- Palette: night navy, magenta, cyan and acid yellow. Magenta is the accent, cyan draws lines and marks.
- Type: the Cyberpunk RUS face on the hero line, OCR-A for headings, IBM Plex Sans for reading.
- Signature: the neon hero line with a cyan glow and a magenta halo.
- Decor: a neon grid, a rain of snowflake, tree and gift emoji, and a garland stripe along the top edge.

Lessons from the original:

- In the brand face the title reads as a glyph puzzle from the back row. Keep it for the hero line, never for body text.
- Snowflakes were emoji in DOM nodes, which the checks read as text. Here the emoji are drawn inside SVG tiles in a pseudo-element.
- Glow on subtitles turns into blur on a projector. Glow belongs to the signature only.
