# Tsagan Sar

Talk: "CI/CD: a love story", Tsagan Sar edition, February. The same talk as the New Year sample, redressed for the Mongolian lunar New Year.

- Palette: cream paper, burgundy, gold, a touch of steppe green and sky blue.
- Type: Playfair Display for headings, Cormorant Garamond for reading.
- Signature: the burgundy band with a gold meander framing every slide.
- Decor: the ornament frame. The original also had petals floating down and a ground squirrel in a hat next to the page number.

Lessons from the original:

- Gold text on cream fails contrast. Gold goes to marks and the frame, headings are burgundy.
- Cormorant runs small and thin. Craft sizes text in slide units, so pick a body face that holds up at those sizes, or set the size on the text roles, not on the slide.
- A mascot by the page number works well as a second signature. Keep it as an image with an empty alt.
