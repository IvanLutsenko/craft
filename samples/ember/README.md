# Ember

Talk: "DevEx in a bank as Stockholm syndrome", a pitch for a conference program committee. No holiday: a calm dark deck that lets the argument lead.

- Palette: charcoal, coral, warm yellow, teal and violet for data.
- Type: Inter for everything, JetBrains Mono for code.
- Signature: the coral-to-yellow gradient on the hero line.
- Decor: one soft warm glow behind the upper middle of the slide.

Lessons from the original:

- Speaker notes lived in the deck and helped in rehearsal. Craft keeps notes out of the release; write them next to the deck.
- The gradient worked because it appeared once. A gradient on every heading stops meaning "the title".
- The deck had no Harold. A pitch without the author on stage can close with `end`.
